# Reading the Three Panes and the Diff

The chapter screen is three columns. Left is what the book says, middle is what Translate has made of it, right is what the Reviewer thinks.

## English source (left)

Your original manuscript for this chapter, unchanged, as the reference. Translate never edits this — it's the yardstick the AI measures the translation against.

## Working text (middle)

The current state of the translation: the newest version any round has produced. Before you run anything, it's the translation you imported. After round 1 it's round 1's output, and so on.

Italics and bold are preserved through every pass, so what you see here is what lands in the Word file.

## Show diff

The tickbox at the top of the middle pane — **"Show diff vs round N"** (or *vs source* on the first round) — switches that pane to a comparison: what changed between the previous version and this one, paragraph by paragraph.

This is the fastest way to judge a round. Instead of re-reading the whole chapter, you read only what the Editor actually touched, and decide whether those changes were improvements.

The tickbox is greyed out when there's nothing to compare against yet.

## Reviewer suggestions (right)

After a Reviewer pass, this pane lists what it flagged — each entry a **quote** from the text and a **comment** about it.

Worth understanding: these are the notes the Editor *received*, not a list of edits it made. The instructions tell the Editor to apply the ones it agrees with, so a chapter with 33 suggestions did not necessarily get 33 changes. Read the diff to see what actually happened.

A falling suggestion count from round to round is a good sign the chapter is settling.

## Moving between chapters

Use the **Chapter** dropdown in the toolbar. Each chapter's status is shown on the book screen:

- **untouched** — no rounds yet
- **in progress** — at least one round done
- **done** — finalized, `final.docx` written
